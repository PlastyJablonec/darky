# Gift filtering

Active gifts are ordered by priority by default, with newest first within the same priority. The owner and visitors can sort by priority, date or price and filter by priority; availability remains hidden for owners of personal surprise lists. Moving either price slider activates the price filter. Quick budgets of CZK 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 5,000 each include a clearly disclosed CZK 100 tolerance above the selected limit; manual slider adjustments use the exact displayed bounds. The price filter applies to one currency at a time, includes zero-priced gifts, and excludes gifts without prices while active. Price sorting places missing prices last; amounts in different currencies are not converted. Received gifts remain in the archive and do not affect the active-list filters or range. AI suggestions receive the complete active list rather than the filtered view.

Run `node test-gift-filters.mjs` to check the priority, availability, currency, price boundary and missing-price behavior.
